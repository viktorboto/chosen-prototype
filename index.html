Dare to Choose · Våga välja

The exercise

Five small steps, straight from the book. By the end you'll have chosen your people — on purpose.

1
List — everyone who matters to you
2
Place — the closest in the centre
3
Evaluate — how do these bonds feel?
4
Reflect — a first note for each
5
Act — reach out to someone today
Why it works

Write down your people

Everyone who matters. Don't filter yet — it's good if the list runs long.

Fill the circles

Tap a name, then tap a ring. The most important go closest to the centre.

Rate each relationship

How satisfied do you feel with each one, 1–10? This sets their starting status.

A first note

Write one line about your closest people. This becomes the first entry in their timeline.

Day one of every story.

Your first nudge

The exercise ends in motion. Here's the first gentle push — and Chosen only ever nudges you once.

Why act now?
Insight fades fast. One small action today turns this exercise into a habit — that's the whole transformation.
