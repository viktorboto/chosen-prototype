Chosen.
Interactive Prototype · Beta MVP

Stay close to the people who matter most.

A Relationship OS — a private, intentional tool that sits on top of the apps you already use. Not a social network. No feed, no likes, no follower counts.

  • ✓Your inner circle of up to 30 people, sorted by relationship health
  • ✓Green / Yellow / Red status, auto-suggested from your touch goals
  • ✓Rituals & gentle nudges — never streaks or red badge counts
  • ✓Real-world actions: send flowers, a handwritten card, a gift
This is a clickable demo. All screens, data and styles are self-contained in one file — explore onboarding, the home loop, a relationship profile, the ritual builder, nudges and settings.
Tip: tap the channel icons, status pills and the + button.
